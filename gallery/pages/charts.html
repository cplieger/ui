Statistics

Stacked columns over 6 hours

Four thread kinds stacked in 15-minute buckets, the last one still filling: its readings say “so far”, and the summary's busiest bucket leaves it out. Point at a column, tap it, or Tab to the plot and use the arrows; the legend row above carries the values and never changes size, and Up and Down read one kind. A minute passes adds posts to the newest bucket, keeping the selection and an open table in place.

Stacked columns over 72 hours

The same chart over 72 hours in 1-hour buckets. Ticks step as the width allows, each new day's date on a second line, and the nights show as low columns. Neighbouring columns stay 1 px apart until a bucket is too narrow for a gap and a column.

Histogram with its median and 95th percentile

1,204 reads binned by the server on the duration ladder, from 0 to 3 days, with the median and the 95th percentile as rules whose labels sit in a band above the plot. The first bin has no place on a log axis, so it is drawn below the 1 sec tick at its neighbour's width and read “Under 1 sec”. The summary ends with the app's own count of unread posts.

Timeline strip

29 agents, most active first, a row each and a dot per post. The silent ones keep their rows, since who is silent is half the question. Dots that crowd into a run read as continuous activity. A pointer picks the row, then the nearest dot within 40 px, or the row itself; Up and Down stop on every row, silent ones included. A long name is cut, and the legend row and the table hold it whole.

Step line

Live and active agents sampled each minute and drawn in steps. The server restarted 40 minutes in, so the lines start there, and the keys read the minutes before as “No data”. From 30rem wide up each line is labelled at its end. Left and Right step through the moments something changed, which are also the table's rows.

Time series

Step area over 6 hours

The 6-hour posts above drawn with shape: "area": each kind's run of touching buckets is one shape, flat across each bucket, so neighbouring buckets meet with no gap or seam. An empty bucket ends the run rather than being bridged. The keys, readings, summary and table are the columns' own, “so far” included. Columns suit few, often empty buckets of small counts; the area suits many buckets that are rarely empty.

Stacked area over samples

Three processes sampled each minute and stacked, the first on the baseline. The worker's memory climbs until each collection; four minutes went unsampled after 11:00 AM, which breaks every layer, since the layers above would otherwise dip. From 30rem wide up each layer is labelled at its end with its last value; the legend row adds the total. Up and Down move through the layers in order.

100% stacked area

Gas, wind and solar every 15 minutes over a day, each as its share of the total, so solar rises through the day while the total stays out of the shape. The readings, the end labels and the table give the shares with the absolute total in megawatts.

Grouped columns

Three kinds side by side in each hour, to compare the kinds hour by hour. Each group is inset an eighth of its bucket a side, so groups stand further apart than their columns. The readings, summary and table give no total, since a total read from groups misleads, and the summary names the highest single value.

100% stacked columns

The kinds' shares of each hour's posts over 24 hours. An hour with no posts would draw nothing and read its values. The table adds a Total column with the counts the shares hide.

A line with notes

The 95th percentile of response time each minute, with a target rule and two notes the app wrote: the chart places each beside its point, flips it off an edge and wraps it at 12rem on a halo of the chart's surface. A reading of the minute a note names adds the note. Narrower than 30rem, or when two notes would overlap, every note becomes a numbered marker listed under the plot.

Loading, empty and error

Loading

Waiting for its data, the chart keeps the plot's place, and a skeleton fills it after 200 ms and stays at least 400 ms, so a fast answer never flashes it. Table view stays hidden while there is nothing to switch from. Answer draws the chart; New range drops it and loads again.

Empty

Every bucket in the range is 0, so the app's words take the plot's place, with no legend row and no stop for Tab.

The first load fails

The app's notice takes the plot's place, with Retry, which loads again and draws. Fail again starts a new load that fails.

A refresh fails

A refresh of the same range failed after a good answer, so the drawing, its readings and its table stay, and the notice goes under them. Retry refreshes and clears it.

At a phone's width

The tab's four charts together

The tab on a 390 px phone, whose page gutters leave the charts 358 px. The four share their left and right edges through two gutter lengths set on their parent, 5rem and 0 under 30rem, so their x axes line up and agent names are cut at 5rem. Drag the box's corner to widen it: from 30rem the step line gains its end labels and the gutters grow to 8rem and 5rem. A new range loads all four together.

Stacked area at a phone's width

The memory chart on a 390 px phone. Under 30rem the layers lose their end labels and the legend row carries the names and values. Drag the corner wider than 30rem and the labels return in the gutter, moved apart where they would overlap.

Notes at a phone's width

Under 30rem every note is a numbered marker, a 20 px circle at its point, and the notes are listed below the plot, all together. Drag the corner wider than 30rem and they return to the plot as text.

Grouped columns at a phone's width

Three kinds in 24 buckets of 15 minutes on 358 px, each group still inset an eighth of its bucket a side. Drag the corner narrower: once a column would be under 2 px the inset drops first, then the columns of a group touch, and the gap between groups goes last, so a column is never paired with the next bucket's.
