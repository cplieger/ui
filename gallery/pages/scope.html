Added from the research

  • Buttons and toolbars: button variants, a button group and split button, a toggle group, and a toolbar that moves what does not fit into More.
  • Prefix and unit: a field with fixed text or a unit at either end.
  • Page header and link tabs: a title with its crumbs and actions, and tabs that are pages.
  • Small confirmation and select panel: a confirm beside its button, and a filterable checklist that applies on Apply.
  • Avatar stack, date range, files, skip link and long names.
  • Layouts: the frames without content at three widths, and the cluster, side-and-main and auto grid arrangements, as Ant Design, Primer, Carbon and Mantine ship theirs.

Left out, and why

Elements other libraries ship that no cplieger app needs yet
ElementWhy it is not here
Calendar grid, date pickerThe native date input brings each platform's calendar and keyboard; the date range uses it.
Carousel, content sliderNo app shows slides. A grid of media cards shows more at once and needs no controls.
Cascader, transfer listNested choices and two-column moves have no use here; picking from suggestions and the ordered list do those jobs.
Colour pickerThe only colour a person chooses is the accent, and Settings offers swatches and a hue for it.
Floating action button, back to topA floating button covers content on a phone, and long lists keep the header in reach.
Hover cardHover alone fails on touch; a toggletip or a link to the item does the job for everyone.
Image viewer, lightboxPosters are the only images, and they are never the thing a person came to see.
Mentions, rich text editorNo app has comments or formatted text. Subflux's sentence editor is its own piece.
One-time code inputThe sign-in library uses passwords, passkeys and single sign-on, with no typed codes.
QR codeA passkey on another device uses the browser's own QR screen.
RatingNo app rates anything; verdicts are words.
Resizable panels, splitterOnly marotte has them, and they stay in marotte.
Tour, coach marksThe first-run states teach in place, and a tour gets in the way of the work.
Tree viewMarotte's file tree stays its own; the other apps nest only two levels, which groups and rows cover.
Watermark, quote, footerTools with a header and a rail have no page footer; the version sits in the shortcut list and Settings.
