Colour steps and tones

Twelve steps per family, the lightness fixed by the stylesheet in each theme and the hue and chroma from the seed. Steps 1 and 2 are surfaces, 3 to 5 control fills, 6 to 8 lines and borders, 9 and 10 solids, 11 and 12 text.

Ladders

Each square is one step, numbered; the last column is the solid's ink.

Icons

Lucide's lines at the language's 2.25-unit stroke, 1.7px at the 18px size, so they stay solid on a 1x screen. Marks drawn inside controls, such as the check, have their own heavier drawing.

Icons with no script

The stylesheet paints every glyph the library's own markup and stylesheet use from its data-cp-icon alone, so a page under a strict content policy that runs no script writes only the span. The frame is such a page, in both themes. Any other glyph takes createIcons or a rule of the app's own, and both win over these.
