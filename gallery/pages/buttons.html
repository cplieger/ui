A link that looks like a button
One primary action per view. Every button presses to 97% under a finger, a mouse or a key. Save changes confirms in place: busy for under a second, then green as the check draws and its words roll to Saved, fading back by itself. An action that confirms in place, such as a row's Grab, plays the same cycle. Delete asks next to itself first, with focus on Keep them, then plays it in its red: Deleting…, then Deleted with its check. The icon-only Refresh turns once a press; it has a name and a tooltip with the same words.

Pin and favourite

A pressed toggle that keeps its state. The pin lifts 2px under a mouse; a press carries it up, then down past its rest as if into a board; pinned, it fills in the accent. The heart beats once and fills when it is on.

Group and split button

Related buttons share edges; a split button keeps its main action one press away and the others in its menu. The main action confirms in place as Save does, at one width; while it works its caret folds into it, and unfolds when it is done.

Toggle group

Pressed buttons for one choice among views; words would make it the segmented control.

Toolbar with More

Drag the toolbar's corner narrower: actions that no longer fit move, in order, into More, and fade back in as room returns. Each action moves as it does everywhere: Refresh turns, Export confirms with a check and keeps its word, Pin drops in and fills.
