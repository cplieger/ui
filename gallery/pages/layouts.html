A logo in the mark

Release tracker
Release tracker
.cp-mark holding an <img> or an inline <svg> in place of its letter: the image fills the mark's box at its own aspect ratio and the letter's fill goes. An svg drawn in currentcolor takes the header's ink, and on a gate page the ink of the name beside it, in both themes. A fixed-colour logo comes as two images, data-cp-scheme="light" and "dark", and the stylesheet shows the one that matches the page's theme, chosen or the system's, with no script. Beside the written name the image takes alt=""; a mark alone takes the name as its alt. Switch the gallery's theme to see the second header's image change.
