Toasts

A toast reports a result the person did not wait for on screen. A passing one drains a thin line and leaves after seven seconds, and a pointer or focus on the stack holds them all; an error stays until dismissed. On touch, a swipe sideways dismisses one.

Kinds

Info and success are polite; warning and error interrupt a screen reader. Each has its glyph, so the tone is never the only signal.

With an action, held, or working

A toast with an action, such as Undo, stays until dismissed. A working toast counts as it goes, offers Open and Cancel, and turns into its result in place.

A whole screen loading

Release tracker Connecting to Sonarr…
Three twirls, each picking its next move at random: a quarter turn either way, a half turn, a full spin as a circle, a hop that rounds in the air and squashes as it lands, or a round swell, with the odd change of colour round the hue wheel. The status line fades in after 1.5 seconds, so a quick load shows only the shapes. Until the script loads, the three run a plain wave. View full screen, then Escape or a click, to close.

Loading content
